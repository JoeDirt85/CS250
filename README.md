## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

I think when it comes to interpreting the needs of a user, theres is a certain skill involved that will take some time to master. Unless you are someone who is in contact with the user, you must think like a user and imagine what you would like to get from the program. The user stories help with this interpretation, because they take a desired feature and break it into three questions: As a (Type of User), I would like to (perform a task) and achieve (some goal). breaking it down this way, gives you, the developer, an idea of who is htis feature for, what action does it perform and what is the outcome.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

In software development, I think to be a good team member, it is very important to be collaborative with others in your team. All while being open to new ideas and the constructive input from those around you. Others may think about finding a solution to a problem in a different manner than you, that could ultimately produce a more efficient or usable program. I think it is very important to be able to hold your own weight, but also being able to seek help from your team when needed.
